Mission & Vision

Engineering evidence,
not engineering claims.

Groundtruth analyzes real project work, GitHub repositories, technical contributions, project-specific AI interviews, coding profiles, and interview-consistent experience evidence — then compresses all of it into a single explainable evidence vector per candidate.

Core philosophy

Don't trust claims. Verify engineering ability through evidence. Every score Groundtruth produces must be traceable back to a commit, a diff, a repository, or a specific answer given in a grounded interview — never a black box.

Problem Statement

Resumes are marketing copy.
Hiring shouldn't run on it.

Technical hiring today is bottlenecked by unverifiable self-reporting, expensive human screening, and interview loops that test performance under pressure — not real engineering ability.

What breaks today

  • Recruiters can't independently verify technical claims at the volume they screen.
  • Take-home tests and whiteboard interviews measure interview skill, not job skill.
  • Strong GitHub profiles get buried under keyword-matched resumes.
  • Engineering managers spend hours on candidates who fail basic technical depth checks.
  • There is no explainable record of *why* a candidate was ranked highly.

What Groundtruth changes

  • Every skill claim is checked against commit history, contribution patterns, and code ownership.
  • AI interviews are generated from the candidate's own repositories — impossible to game generically.
  • Coding profile and experience evidence corroborate (or contradict) the resume automatically.
  • Recruiters search and rank on a single explainable evidence vector, not a gut feeling.
  • Every hiring decision ships with a traceable evidence trail.
